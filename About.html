Connecting University of Eldoret Students with Local Suppliers

Our Goal

We aim to make it easier for University of Eldoret students to find and access information about nearby water depots, gas suppliers and electronics shops. Our platform brings the most relevant supplier information together in one place, saving you time and effort.

Browse a category or search, open a supplier to see where it is and what it offers, then call or message them directly.
